Mechanism of DNA Replication: 3D Molecular Animation

Observe the real-time choreography of enzymes at the replication fork — unwinding, leading and lagging strand synthesis, and Okazaki fragment maturation.

Phase 1 Unwinding Helicase separates parental strands; topoisomerase relieves supercoiling.
Phase 2 Priming RNA primase deposits short primers; SSBs prevent reannealing.
Phase 3 Synthesis DNA Polymerase builds 5'→3' (continuous leading; discontinuous lagging).
Phase 4 Maturation Primers removed, DNA filled, and DNA ligase seals phosphodiester nicks.
