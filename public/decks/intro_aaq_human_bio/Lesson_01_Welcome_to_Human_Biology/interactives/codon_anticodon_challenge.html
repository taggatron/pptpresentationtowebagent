🧬 RNA Base Pairing Rules

During Translation, the 3 bases of the mRNA codon pair with the 3 complementary bases of the tRNA anticodon via hydrogen bonding:

Adenine (A) ↔ Uracil (U)
Linked by 2 Hydrogen bonds
Cytosine (C) ↔ Guanine (G)
Linked by 3 Hydrogen bonds

⚠️ Key OCR Examiner Tip: Thymine (T) NEVER appears in mRNA or tRNA. Always use Uracil (U)!
