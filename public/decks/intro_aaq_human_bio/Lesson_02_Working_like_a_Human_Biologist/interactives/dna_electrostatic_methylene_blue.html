🔬 Staining Mechanism in Human Cytology

Methylene Blue ($C_{16}H_{18}ClN_3S$) is a heterocyclic aromatic cationic dye. Its quaternary phenothiazinium ring carries a delocalized net positive charge (+1).

The DNA double helix is a polyanion: each repeating nucleotide contains a negatively charged phosphodiester residue ($-1$). This establishes an intense negative electrostatic field in the minor groove.

Methylene Blue +1.00 e / MB⁺ Deep Blue (Cationic)

Clinical Takeaway: Cell nuclei contain dense genomic chromatin with millions of negative phosphate residues, attracting concentrated $MB^+$ and staining dark blue, while protein-rich cytoplasm stains faintly.
