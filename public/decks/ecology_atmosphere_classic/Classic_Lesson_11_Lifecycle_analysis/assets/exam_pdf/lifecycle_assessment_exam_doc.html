1 Life cycle assessment of consumer electronics

A life cycle assessment assesses the environmental impact of products such as smartphones. Some activities in the life cycle of a smartphone are listed below.

  • Extracting oil from oil wells
  • Packing the phone in a box
  • Making plastics from oil
  • Watching videos on the phone
  • Obtaining copper from copper ore
  • Taking apart the phone
  • Putting together all the components
  • Recycling the copper from some components
Describe the four stages that are assessed in a life cycle assessment.
In your answer, give at least one example from the list above for each stage.
✍️ Write your answer in continuous prose. Clearly identify each stage and link at least one activity from the list. [6 Marks]

📋 Indicative Scientific Content (The 4 LCA Stages & Examples)
⛏️ Stage 1: Extracting & Processing Raw Materials
Matching examples from list:
• Extracting oil from oil wells (crude oil for polymers/plastics).
• Obtaining copper from copper ore (mining, smelting, electrolysis).
Impacts: Mining ores, drilling oil, quarrying; causes habitat destruction, deforestation, toxic runoff, heavy machinery fossil fuel use.
🏭 Stage 2: Manufacturing & Packaging
Matching examples from list:
• Making plastics from oil (refining and cracking).
• Putting together all the components (assembling the smartphone).
• Packing the phone in a box (cardboard/packaging).
Impacts: High thermal and electrical energy consumption, industrial chemical emissions, wastewater, factory carbon emissions.
📱 Stage 3: Use & Operation Over Lifetime
Matching example from list:
• Watching videos on the phone (charging and powering the device).
Impacts: Cumulative electricity drawn from the grid during recharging, battery life degradation, data-centre energy transmission.
♻️ Stage 4: Disposal & End of Life
Matching examples from list:
• Taking apart the phone (dismantling, sorting parts).
• Recycling copper from phone components (metal recovery).
Impacts: Landfill space, non-biodegradable electronic waste, heavy metals leaching into groundwater, toxic emissions if incinerated.
Examiner Guidance & Common Pitfalls:
• Transport: Transport and distribution occurs across all four stages (transporting crude oil, shipping parts, distributing to shops, hauling waste to landfill). Do NOT accept transport as a distinct fifth stage.
• Recycling: Recycling the copper is a disposal/reprocessing activity that mitigates the need to extract fresh raw materials in Stage 1.
• Scoris Annotations: Annotate with L1, L2, L3 level descriptors and clear ticks on matched activity examples.
