🔴 1. Changes on Mars
  • Carbon Dioxide: Increased proportion to become 95% of the modern atmosphere.
  • Water Vapour: Decreased dramatically to trace levels.
  • Oxygen: Small trace amounts have appeared.
  • Reason / Mechanism: Mars cooled drastically to an average of -55 °C; water vapour condensed and froze into polar ice caps and underground permafrost.
🌍 2. Comparison with Earth & Scientific Reasons
  • Carbon Dioxide: Decreased drastically on Earth from ~95% to 0.04%.
  • Oceans Formed: As Earth cooled, water vapour condensed into liquid oceans.
  • CO₂ Dissolved: Vast amounts of CO₂ dissolved into oceans and reacted to form insoluble carbonate precipitates & sedimentary rocks (e.g. limestone).
  • Photosynthesis: Algae and green plants evolved, performing photosynthesis (6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂), absorbing CO₂ and releasing oxygen up to 21%.
  • Nitrogen: Accumulated to 78% because N₂ is chemically inert and unreactive.
