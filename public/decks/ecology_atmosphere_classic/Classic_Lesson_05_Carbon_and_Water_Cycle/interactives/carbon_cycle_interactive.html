The Carbon Cycle Overview
Biological Chemical

Carbon is the fundamental backbone of all organic molecules (carbohydrates, fats, proteins, and DNA). Because the Earth is a closed system with finite carbon, it must be continuously recycled between the atmosphere, living organisms, and long-term geological reservoirs.

Key Rule: Photosynthesis takes CO₂ OUT.
Respiration, Decomposition & Combustion put it IN.

⚡ Trace the Carbon Atom

Follow a single carbon atom through its journey over 300 million years:

1

Atmosphere: CO₂ Gas

Floating in air, trapped by chlorophyll.

2

Producer: Oak Leaf Glucose

Converted by light energy during photosynthesis.

3

Consumer: Caterpillar Muscle

Leaf eaten; carbon built into insect protein.

4

Soil: Decomposition

Insect dies; fungi respire releasing some CO₂.

5

Fossil Fuel: Compressed Coal

Buried biomass trapped under heat & pressure.

6

Combustion: Power Station

Burned with oxygen, roaring back into the air!
