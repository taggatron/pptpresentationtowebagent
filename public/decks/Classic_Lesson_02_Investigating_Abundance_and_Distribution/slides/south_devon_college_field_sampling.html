1 Why use Satellite GIS before stepping outside?
▼
Satellite imagery allows ecologists to establish exact boundary coordinates ($L \times W$) and calculate total habitat area ($4,950\text{ m}^2$) safely and precisely before setting up field tape measures.
2 Campus Pitch Edge Effects & Footpaths
▼
Soil along pitch sidelines is heavily compacted by students walking between buildings. This alters dandelion growth vs. rye grass. A truly random coordinate system prevents investigators from subconsciously oversampling comfortable paths!
3 Assumption: Is the field uniform?
▼
Our scaling multiplier assumes the pitch has consistent sunlight and soil drainage. If the northern boundary is shaded by trees or campus buildings, a belt transect must be used to sample the environmental gradient.
