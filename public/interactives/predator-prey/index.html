NATURAL DYNAMICS

One ecosystem. Two lenses.

Population model traces idealised, continuous populations. Prey reproduce; predators eat prey, reproduce and die. The arena illustrates the graph, rather than driving it.

Agent model tracks individuals. Prey move and reproduce; predators seek nearby prey and may starve. Chance and encounters create a less regular pattern.

The pairings illustrate a simplified two-species relationship. Real ecosystems contain many species, migration, changing resources and other pressures. These are teaching models, not forecasts.

Try this: observe one cycle, predict the effect of a disturbance, then explain the graph. The axes rescale as populations change. Time is in model units, not years.
