GCSE Method & Observations
1
Turn on motor. Rubber belt carries electrons down, leaving dome with high net positive charge.
2
Electric field strength increases until potential difference exceeds 30 kV/cm.
3
Air molecules ionise into conductive plasma; spark jumps to earthed sphere, equalising charge.
4
With pie tins: Like charges repel each other, launching tins off the dome sequentially!
Core Physics Equations
Q = I × t
Charge (C) = Current (A) × Time (s)
Law of Charges
Like charges repel (+ and +, - and -)
Unlike charges attract (+ and -)
Safety Considerations

Always keep earthing lead connected to mains earth or generator base. People with pacemakers or cardiac conditions must remain 3m clear.
