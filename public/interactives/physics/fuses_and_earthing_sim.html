Plug Wiring Standard (BS 1363)
Live (Brown): 230 V AC. Connected to Fuse.
Neutral (Blue): ~0 V. Completes circuit.
Earth (Green/Yellow): 0 V safety to metal chassis.
Core Equations
Current Calculation (Ohm / Power)
I = P ÷ V
E.g. 2300 W ÷ 230 V = 10 A → Use 13 A fuse
Fault Heating & Fuse Melting
P = I² × R
Earth short circuit gives massive I (>100 A), melting thin fuse wire instantly.
Investigation Steps
1
Select the 2300 W Kettle. Notice current is 10 A. Test with a 3 A or 5 A fuse — observe the fuse blow instantly!
2
Install the correct 13 A fuse. Turn power ON. The kettle element heats up normally.
3
Toggle Live Wire Fault with Earth connected. Observe the huge current surge to ground melting the fuse, rendering the appliance safe!
4
Disconnect the Earth wire with the fault active. The chassis is now energized at 230 V! Toggle "Person Touching Case" to demonstrate lethal electric shock!
