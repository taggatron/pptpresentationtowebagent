3

4
Adjust variable resistor to vary resistance and observe effect on lamp brightness.
Formulas & Definitions
Q = I × t
Charge (C) = Current (A) × Time (s)
V = E / Q
Potential Difference (V) = Energy (J) / Charge (C)
P = V × I
Power (W) = Voltage (V) × Current (A)
