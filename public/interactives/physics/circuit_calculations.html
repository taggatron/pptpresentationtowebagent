GCSE Electrical Formulas
V = I × R
Voltage (V) = Current (A) × Resistance (Ω)
P = V × I
Power (W) = Voltage (V) × Current (A)
P = I² × R
Power (W) = Current² (A²) × Resistance (Ω)
E = P × t
Energy (J) = Power (W) × Time (s)
E = Q × V
Energy (J) = Charge (C) × Voltage (V)
