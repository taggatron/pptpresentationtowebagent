Series Circuit Rules
• Current is the same everywhere:
I_total = I₁ = I₂ = I₃
• Potential Difference is shared:
V_total = V₁ + V₂
• Resistance adds up:
R_total = R₁ + R₂
• If one lamp breaks, the circuit opens and all lamps go out.
Parallel Circuit Rules
• Current splits across branches:
I_total = I₁ + I₂
• Potential Difference is equal across every branch:
V_total = V₁ = V₂
• Total Resistance decreases as more branches are added.
• If one lamp breaks, other branches stay lit at full brightness!
Domestic Application
Household sockets and lights are wired in parallel so each appliance receives the full mains 230 V and can be switched independently.
