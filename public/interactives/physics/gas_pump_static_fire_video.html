Stop Static: Refuelling Fire at Gas Pump

Investigating how friction between clothing and car upholstery generates thousands of volts of static charge, causing a spark discharge that ignites petrol vapour.

Phase 1 Friction & Charge Sliding on synthetic seat transfers electrons, charging driver up to ~20,000 V.
Phase 2 Vapour Cloud Petrol evaporates quickly, forming a volatile flammable vapour envelope.
Phase 3 Spark Discharge Touching metal nozzle triggers a dielectric spark, igniting the fumes.
Phase 4 Earthing & Safety Touch metal chassis (earthing) first. Never pull out a burning nozzle!
